# Sample move images

The three photos in this directory are used for the thriv.es Movers "sample move" estimator demo. All three were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and resized (long side 1600 px, recompressed as JPEG) for the thriv.es Movers sample move. No other changes were made. They depict no real customer and are not affiliated with thriv.es.
